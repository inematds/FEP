1. Zero-Shot Prompting

Direct instruction, no examples

Zero-Shot = ask for something without giving examples. Works for common tasks the model saw during training.

"Classify the sentiment as Positive, Negative, or Neutral:

'I love the product!'"

✅ When to use

Simple tasks, obvious format

⚠️ Limitation

May not capture a specific style

📚

2. Few-Shot Prompting

2-5 examples to guide the pattern

Show input → output examples before your task. The model identifies the pattern and replicates it.

// Examples

Text: "João Silva is a director." → Name: João Silva | Title: Director

Text: "Maria Santos, CEO." → Name: Maria Santos | Title: CEO

// Your task

Text: "Pedro Lima, coordinator." → ?

💡 Tip

Use 3-5 representative examples and keep the format consistent

🧠

3. Chain of Thought (CoT)

"Think out loud"

Ask the model explain your reasoning step by step. Dramatically improves results in logic and math.

"Think step by step before answering."

❌ No CoT

Model may jump to the wrong answer

✅ With CoT

Explicit reasoning, +40% accuracy

🎭

4. Role Prompting

"You are an expert in..."

Defining a role adjusts vocabulary, tone, depth, and perspective automatically.

👨‍💼 Consultant

"You are a consultant with 20 years of experience..."

👨‍🏫 Teacher

"You are a teacher explaining things to beginners..."

✍️

5. Text Humanization

Avoiding an “AI tone”

Techniques for making text sound more natural: contractions, opinions, structural variation, colloquial expressions.

❌ AI tone

"It is undoubtedly important to consider..."

✅ Humanized

"In my experience, it's well worth it..."

↻

6. Iterate with Intention

Turn the first response into a better result

Choose a technique from this module, test the prompt, and create a second version by changing just one variable: examples, role, format, or tone.

Before You Submit

Define the expected result and the criteria for evaluating the response.

After the response

Record what improved, what got worse, and what the next isolated change will be.
